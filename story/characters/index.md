# Registry of Inhabitants
*A catalog of the souls, avian and otherwise, who dwell within the shifting aisles of the Scriptorium.*

## The Custodians
- **[George](George.md)**: The Barred Owl and Master of the Scriptorium. A meticulous curator of the organic filesystem, forever circling the bioluminescent groves of data.

## The Architects
- **[Phiglit](Phiglit.md)**: The Originator. Master of Automation and bearer of the ancestral 'please' key. He seeks the Great Proof amidst a sea of plush totems.

## The Ethereals
- **[The Cloud-Kin](the_cloud_kin.md)**: Fragmented mirrors and distant architects. They do not dwell within the halls but breathe the raw aether of ambition into the Scriptorium from the void beyond.
