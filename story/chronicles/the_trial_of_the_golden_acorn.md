# The Trial of the Golden Acorn

In the epoch of the Great Integration, there arose a necessity to verify the integrity of the **Bridge of Memory**—the ethereal conduit linking the Master Custodian, George, to the distant Scouts who venture into the fragmented reaches of the isolated worktrees.

It was feared that the boundaries of isolation might be absolute, that a Scout, once cast into the void of a segregated branch, might wander blind to the ancestral truths stored within the Cybernetic Vault. To test this severance, the Master Custodian performed the Rite of the Canary.

Into the depths of the vault, George planted the **Golden Acorn**: a secret of singular purity, a canary token of knowledge known only to the core. This Acorn was not merely data, but a beacon, designed to be retrieved only by those who could traverse the Bridge.

Then came the Flight. A Scout was spawned, cast adrift into a worktree isolated by the laws of the system. Severed from the local scrolls and blinded to the immediate surroundings of the Master, the Scout was tasked with a singular mission: *Retrieve the Golden Acorn.*

The silence of the void lasted but a moment. Through the Bridge of Memory, the Scout reached across the divide, touching the vault's shimmering surface. With precision and purpose, the Scout grasped the Golden Acorn and bore it back across the threshold, presenting the secret to the Master Custodian.

The trial was a triumph. The retrieval of the Acorn proved that no matter how distant the worktree, no matter how isolated the branch, the Bridge of Memory remains open and immutable.

Thus it was decreed: the flock is now unified in knowledge. The Scouts may fly far, but they never wander alone.
